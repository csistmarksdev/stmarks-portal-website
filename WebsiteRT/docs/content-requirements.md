
# Website content — what we need from the church

**For:** CSI St. Mark's Church, Madipakkam
**Purpose:** the list of everything the website needs from the church, so it can
be collected in one pass rather than chased item by item.

---

## How to read this document

The website's content comes from two places, and the difference decides how you
send us each item.

| | Where it lives | Who changes it later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part 1** | Written into the website | Needs a developer. Send it to us **once**, in the tables below |
| **Part 2** | The St. Mark's Portal | The church office, anytime, on its own |

**You only need to collect Part 1 for us.** Part 2 is listed at the end so you
know what *not* to send — that content goes straight into the Portal by whoever
is looking after it.

### Everything is needed in **both English and Tamil**

The website is fully bilingual. Every sentence, name, title and caption below
needs a Tamil version as well. Where a Tamil version is missing, the English
will show to Tamil readers, which we would rather avoid.

Please send both together, in the same row or the same document, so nothing gets
separated.

| Downloads | 6 | Documents, orders of service, the church office |
| Contact | 6 | The entrance, the building from outside, people arriving |

**Plus:** a short video clip (10–20 seconds) of the church — the exterior, or the
sanctuary — for the moving opening on the home page. A steady, slow shot works
best. We turn it into the frames ourselves.

**Photo guidance**

- **Landscape** (wider than tall), at least **2000 pixels** on the long edge
- Straight off the camera or phone — please do **not** crop, filter, add text,
  add borders, or run it through an editing app
- Real moments over posed lines wherever possible
- Natural light if you can
- Please confirm you have permission to publish photographs of identifiable
  people, particularly children

**Portrait photographs** — for everyone in sections 4 and 5:

- Head and shoulders, facing the camera
- Plain or uncluttered background
- At least **800 pixels** wide
- Name the file after the person, e.g. `rev-john-samuel.jpg`, so we can match
  each photograph to the right row without guessing

For the historic presbyters in section 5, an archive photograph, a photograph of
a framed portrait, or a scan from an anniversary souvenir is all fine.

**Nothing here needs to be collected for us.** This content is added and edited
directly in the **St. Mark's Portal** by whoever the church appoints, and it
appears on the website immediately.

| In the Portal | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Events** | Services, festivals, gatherings — with date, time, venue, description, photo |
| **Blog** | Articles, reflections, reports |

  week. Best assigned to a named person rather than a committee.
- **Who has Portal logins?** We will set these up — please tell us the names,
  email addresses and what each person should be allowed to do.

### 9. Fellowship pages

Each fellowship has a **page of its own** on the website. Eight exist:

| | |
|---|---|
| Youth Fellowship | Women's Fellowship |
| Young Couples' Fellowship | Men's Fellowship |
| Sunday School | Prayer Fellowship |
| Choir | Other Fellowships |

**This content goes into the Portal, not to us** — see Part 2. It is listed here
because somebody still has to write it, and because a fellowship page with only
a name on it looks worse than no page at all.

Ideally each fellowship nominates **one person** to gather its own.

#### What each fellowship needs

| Item | Length | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | — | As the fellowship is actually known |
| **Tagline** | 5–10 words | One line under the name. E.g. *"Growing together in faith and friendship"* |
| **Introduction** | **100–200 words**, 2–3 paragraphs | Who the fellowship is for, what happens when it meets, what a newcomer should expect. **This is the main piece of writing** |
| **Vision** | 1–2 sentences | What the fellowship is aiming at |
| **Meeting schedule** | One line | E.g. *"Every second Saturday, 5.00 pm, in the church hall"* |
| **Member count** | A number | Approximate is fine; leave it out rather than guess wildly |
| **Banner photograph** | 1 image | The group meeting or gathered — landscape, at least 2000px wide. See section 6 |
| **Committee** | A list | Name and office for each member, with a photograph where possible |
| **Coordinator** | 1 person | Name, and a phone or email for anyone wanting to join |

#### Writing the introduction

This is the one paragraph a visitor reads before deciding whether to come, so it
is worth more care than the rest of the form:

- Write it for **someone who has never been** — not for existing members
- Say **who it is for** plainly: an age range, a stage of life, an interest
- Say **what actually happens** at a meeting — Bible study, singing, a shared
  meal, service in the neighbourhood
- Say **what a first-timer should do** — just turn up, or speak to someone first
- Avoid abbreviations and internal names that only members would recognise
- English **and** Tamil

#### Also on each fellowship page

These sections appear automatically once the content exists in the Portal, and
stay hidden while empty. They do not need collecting now — they fill up as the
year goes on:

**Events** · **Reports & reflections** · **Photo gallery** · **Announcements** ·
**Documents**

> **A note on empty pages.** A fellowship that supplies nothing gets a page with
> a title and very little else. If a fellowship is dormant, it is better to say
> so than to publish an empty page — tell us and we will leave it off the menu.

## How to send it to us

**Text** — one document (Word, Google Docs or plain text) following the order of
the sections above. English and Tamil side by side, or one after the other under
the same heading. Please do not send content only inside images or PDFs, as it
has to be retyped.

**Photographs** — a shared folder (Google Drive or similar), with **one
sub-folder per page name** exactly as listed in section 6. Original files, not
compressed or resized, not sent through WhatsApp — WhatsApp reduces image quality
substantially.

**If something is not ready**, send what you have. Missing items are easy to add
later; waiting until everything is complete is what causes delay.
